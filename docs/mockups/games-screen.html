Try it

  • Search for zelda past or pokemon. Word order, accents and punctuation don't matter, and version names match too.
  • Click a system's name to list only its games (Ctrl-click adds another). Click its box to take or drop the whole system.
  • Tick a game, then Shift-click another box: everything in between follows.
  • With a search or filter on, the box in the list header acts on every game shown. Undo is in the message that pops up.
  • On Miyoo SD, tick PSP. The card overflows and Apply is blocked with the reason.
  • Click a row to see why a game is in or out, tick it on other outputs, or change its version for every output.
  • Press Simulate new games. Miyoo SD takes the new GBA games because GBA is ticked, and leaves out the SNES ones because its SNES games were picked by hand.
  • Switch Choosing for to see another output's ticks, then press Apply.

Decided for this design

  • Each output has its own ticks; a game's version is shared by every output.
  • Ticks save at once; output folders change only on Apply.
  • New games follow their system's box, or the output's default when the system has none.

Games, sizes and folders are made up. The full plan is docs/game-selection-plan.md in the repository.
